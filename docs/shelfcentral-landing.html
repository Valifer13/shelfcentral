FAQ

Common questions

Is Shelfcentral suitable for schools and universities?

Absolutely. Shelfcentral is purpose-built for educational institutions. Schools and universities can manage unlimited students as members, set role-based access for librarians and administrators, and use the analytics dashboard to track reading engagement across departments.

Does it support barcode systems?

Yes. Each book copy gets a unique barcode. Staff can use any standard USB scanner to check out or return books instantly. Labels can be printed directly from the platform.

Can each library manage its own data independently?

Yes. Shelfcentral uses a multi-tenant architecture where each library has a completely isolated data environment. Your members, books, and logs are never accessible to other institutions on the platform.

Is there a self-service member dashboard?

Yes. Members get their own login to view borrowings and due dates, check fine balances, manage reservations, and browse the catalog — all without staff involvement.

Can fines be automated?

Yes. You define fine policies — daily rates per category, grace periods, and caps — and Shelfcentral enforces them automatically. Fines accrue the moment a loan goes overdue, visible to staff and the borrower in real time.
